Hello everyone,

As 2022 comes to an end, I find myself writing my very first newsletter. This has been a challenging year, but one filled with long awaited moments. Many of you have been a big part of my progression over the seasons, so I'm thrilled to finally share some of these stories, memories, and results with you.

## The year in short

The year started like it did for most of you, with a two month lockdown. I kept my head up, and when it ended I was finally able to sail again in Florida at the Midwinters East regatta. As the lake unfroze back home, the Montréal season began and I got to work on my weakest skills: downwind and reaching.

Summer brought the regattas. I won the local events, but internationally I met a new challenge: my level dropped at moments of peak pressure, and my results at the North Americans and US Nationals fell short of my goals. With that disappointment fueling me, I doubled down on my preparation for the World Championship.

## My first World Championship, Vilamoura

My Worlds build up was a two week camp in Valencia followed by a pre-worlds camp in Vilamoura, and it paid off: I arrived ready, mentally and physically. This was one of my most long awaited moments, having missed two Optimist Worlds to the lockdowns. I sailed my best but missed the gold fleet by 2 points. Still, I left Vilamoura with more than I came with, and with a new life set up in Portugal, I was hungry for what came next.

## A strong finish

After two months of training in my new home base, I won the Portuguese national championship as a Canadian sailor, my second win back to back. I felt on top of the world, which changed quickly at the 46th Christmas Race in Palamós, Spain: good sailing, but some critical tactical mistakes left me 6th overall. A fair first comparison against the European fleet, and plenty to work on.

## What's ahead in 2023

A big training block, the ILCA 6 ISAF Youth Worlds qualifier in St. Petersburg, Florida, then another block building toward the ILCA 4 European Championship in Cádiz, Spain.

Thank you for reading my first newsletter. I wish you all the best for the new year, and I look forward to seeing you in 2023.

— Carlos
