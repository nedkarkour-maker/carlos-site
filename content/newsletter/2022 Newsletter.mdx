Hello everyone !

As 2022 comes to an end, I now find myself writing my first newsletter. This has been a challenging year but definitely filled with long awaited events. As many of you have been a big part of my career progression over the seasons, I am thrilled to finally share with you some of these stories, memories, and of course my progression over the past year.

Let's first start with a quick recap:

January and February: Training base in Cascais + Waszp clinic in Pensecola
March and April: florida + season start in montreal
May and June: spring training
July and august: North American Circuit + World Championship
September and Octobre: Training in Portugal
November and December: Portuguese regatta + Palamos Christmas Regatta

I started my year like most of you with a 2 month lock down. I keeped my head up and my spirits high as it came to an end, therefore I was finally able to sail down in florida, at which I participated in the Mid Winter East regatta. Shortly after as the lake unfroze the Montreal sailing season had begun. I was back hard at work improving on my weaker skill: downwind and reach. 

Then summer approached and all the regattas started. I won the local events but encountered so many new challenges internationally. I saw my sailing level decrease at the times of peak pressure, which meant my results at the North American and US nationals did not exceed my goals. With disappointment and anger on my mind I decided to work even hard towards my preparation for the World Championship.

My Worlds training consisted of a camp in Valencia for 2 weeks then a pre-worlds camp in Vilamoura. Those really paid off as I was definitely ready for the Worlds both mentally and physically. As I found unity with my boat I experienced my first world championship. This was one of my most long awaited moments as I had previously missed out on 2 optimist world championships because of the lockdown. I sailed as best I could but sadly missed out on the gold felt by 2 points. I left vilamoura with more than I came, so overall I was very happy and looking forward to my futur training as I moved to Portugal.

With a new life set up in Portugal I got back into the gym and on the water, with two months of training I competed at the first Portuguese national championship and proudly won as a canadian sailor. I felt on top of the world as I won two events back to back, that quickly changed as I headed to palamos spain for the 46th christmas regatta. Throughout that event I sailed very well but made some critical tactical mistakes. Those lead me to 6th overall, I was still happy with the results as this was my first comparaison to the European fleet since the world championship.



With 2023 on the horizon here is what is coming up:

The next two months consist of a big taining block as well as the Ilca 6 ISAF youth worlds qualifier in Saint-Pete, Florida. After that I will have another big training block in preparation for the Ilca 4 European Championship in Cadiz, Spain. 

As we transition into the new year I wish you all the best, I thank you for reading my newsletter.
I look forward to seeing you soon again in 2023.

- Carlos Charabati
