The week came down to staying methodical when it mattered. Viana do Castelo threw big Atlantic swell and gusty afternoons at the fleet, and the racing rewarded exactly what we'd trained for: clean starts, disciplined lanes, no hero moves.

## The moment

The final race, the maths said "just finish in the top group." The hardest thing in sailing is doing *less* than you can — sailing the percentages when every instinct says attack.

> Measure, adjust, repeat. It works on a spreadsheet, and it works in a world championship.

This title belongs to a long list of people — my family, my coach, Clube Naval de Cascais, and everyone back in Montréal who kept this project alive.

The real work starts now.
